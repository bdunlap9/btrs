Daily readings Temperature and humidity, one card per day

Enter temperature and relative humidity and this works out grains per pound for you. Grain depression across the dehumidifier tells you it is actually pulling water. Affected air trending down day over day tells you the structure is releasing it. This is the record that justifies every equipment day on the invoice.
