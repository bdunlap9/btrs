Payment schedule

On a lump-sum job in Florida you pay tax on materials at the register and quote one price, rather than adding tax on top of the customer's invoice. The customer copy folds it into the material line for that reason. Confirm with your accountant for how you are set up.

Loss guard The two numbers that keep a job from going underwater

The first one is a hard floor. If the scope, rates and markups ever produce a price below your cost plus that margin, the tool raises the price and tells you. The second one pads every material price still marked unchecked, so an unverified price book quotes high instead of low. Tick a price off as checked and its padding drops away.
