The formulas

Air movers (S500-2021): one air mover in each affected room, plus one for every 50–70 ft² of wet floor, plus one for every 100–150 ft² of wet ceiling and upper wall above the bottom two feet, plus one for each wall inset or offset greater than 18 inches.

Refrigerant dehumidifiers: cubic feet ÷ chart factor = required PPD; required PPD ÷ AHAM rating = number of units.

Desiccant and AFDs: cubic feet × ACH ÷ 60 = required CFM; required CFM ÷ unit CFM = number of units.
